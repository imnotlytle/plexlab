# Plex setup

You've got access to a private Plex server called **patplex v2** — Movies, TV Shows, and
Audiobooks. It works from anywhere, not just at the house.

**1. Accept the invite.** You'll get an email from Plex. Click the link, or go to
[app.plex.tv](https://app.plex.tv) and accept it there. Sign in with your Plex account or make one —
it's free, and you don't need Plex Pass.

**2. Install the Plex app.**

| Device | Get it |
|---|---|
| iPhone / iPad / Apple TV | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/plex-stream-movies-shows/id383457673) |
| Android / Google TV / Chromecast | [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.plexapp.android) |
| Roku, Fire TV, LG, Samsung, Xbox, PlayStation | Search "Plex" in the device's own app store — full list at [plex.tv/apps-devices](https://www.plex.tv/apps-devices/) |
| Computer | Nothing to install — use [app.plex.tv](https://app.plex.tv) |

**3. Sign in and pick the server.** Use the same account you accepted the invite with. In the
sidebar, choose **patplex v2**. The libraries are under it.

**4. Set up Overseerr — this is how you ask for stuff that isn't there.** Go to
[overseerr.patplex.net](https://overseerr.patplex.net) and click **Sign In with Plex**. Same
account, no new password. Search for a movie or show and hit **Request** — for a series you can
take the whole thing or just some seasons. It downloads and appears in Plex on its own, usually
within a few hours. Overseerr also tells you if something's already on the server, so check there
first.

There's no Overseerr app — it's a website. On your phone, open it in the browser and use Share →
Add to Home Screen and it behaves like one.

Done — press play.

---

**If it buffers:** in the app, Settings → Video Quality → lower the remote streaming quality.

**If anything else is off:** text Pat.
